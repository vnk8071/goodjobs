How Good Jobs works

1. Search once

Enter a job title, a skills list or paste your CV. AI maps it to a canonical keyword and detects the right market.

2. Stream results

Each job board is scraped live and results stream in as they arrive, so you do not wait for the slowest source.

3. Compare and apply

See title, company, location, skills and a match score. Every listing links back to the original posting.

Sources we aggregate

Vietnam: LinkedIn, ITviec, TopCV, VietnamWorks, CareerViet, CareerLink, TopDev, JobsGo, Glints, ViecOi, Indeed VN, TechJobs, Xóm Jobs and Google Careers. Global: LinkedIn, Indeed, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, Glassdoor, USAJOBS and Dice.

Who is Good Jobs for?

Good Jobs is built for candidates who want to compare several job boards without opening each one. It is useful if you are looking for your first job, moving from a junior to a senior role, returning to the market after a break, or exploring remote work while living in Vietnam. Employers and headhunters can also submit a role directly for review.

Remote and global roles

Remote work is a first-class location, not a filter afterthought. Pick "Remote" to see roles from RemoteOK, We Work Remotely and companies that hire across time zones. For global markets, type a city such as Singapore, London or New York and Good Jobs infers the country and selects the matching sources. Salaries and requirements stay in the original posting, so you always apply at the source.

Job search tips

Use English job titlesMost multinationals and IT teams post in English — "Data Scientist" returns more than the Vietnamese title.
Add a levelInclude "Senior", "Junior" or "Intern" to narrow results by experience.
Try "Remote"Set the location to Remote to see roles that do not require office attendance.
Paste your CVLet AI read your skills and suggest the closest job keyword instead of guessing.
