Contact Good Jobs

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns about our job search aggregation service, we would love to hear from you.

Please reach out to us by email at:

khoivn@goodjobs.io.vn

What we can help with

Job seekers: report a broken listing, a wrong apply link, or a search result that does not match the role. Include the job title and the URL you were viewing.

Employers and headhunters: submit a role through the Post a Job form, or email us if you need help with a submission, a correction or a removal.

Partnerships and data sources: if you run a job board and want your listings considered for aggregation, tell us about your site and the markets you cover.

Privacy requests: for questions about the data described in our Privacy Policy, contact us with the subject "Privacy request".

Response time

We aim to reply within two business days. For urgent listing corrections, include "Urgent" in the subject line and the direct URL so we can find the record quickly.

Other ways to reach us

You can also follow updates and contact us through our LinkedIn page. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or payment details by email.

For Employers / Job Boards:

If you are a job board or employer whose listings appear on Good Jobs and you would like to have them removed or discuss an integration partnership, please email us directly with the subject line "Employer Request".
